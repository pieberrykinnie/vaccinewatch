# Contributing Guidelines

## Branching

Four layers:

1. `main` stable branch and what we will use in our final submission
1. `dev` for merging working features
1. `mobile`/`backend`/`bridge`/`watch` for each component of our project
1. `feat`/`fix`/`docs` branches **must** be from the third group of branches

## Pull Requests

- Merging from Group 4 to Group 3: It's your branch, do whatever.
- Merging from Group 3 to Group 2: **Must** require at least one external review.
- Merging from Group 2 to Group 1: **Must** require the **full** group review.
- All PRs of different manners will be closed immediately.
